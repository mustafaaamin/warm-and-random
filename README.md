# Partile and Wave Dark Matter Volterra Codes

Mathematica notebooks accompanying a series of papers on particle and wave dark matter. Please cite the relevant paper if you use or adapt this code.

## Included codes and corresponding papers

- **ADM25_particle_single-species.nb** – Particle case, single species: Amin, Delos & Mirbabayi (2025)[https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.20881]
- **AMM25_wave_single-species.nb** – Wave case, single species:  Amin, May & Mirbabayi (2025) [https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.12131]
- **ADY25_particle_multi-species.nb** – Particle case, multiple species: Amin, Delos & Yang (2025) [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.15046]
- **AD25_waves_multi-species.nb** – Particle case, multiple species: Amin & Delos (2025) [https://arxiv.org/abs/2510.17977]

Each folder contains Mathematica notebooks used to compute Volterra solutions, and dark matter matter power spectra for the corresponding scenarios.
